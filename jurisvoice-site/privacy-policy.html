Introduction

JOBLYT LLC ("Company", "JurisVoice", or "We") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit the website https://www.JurisVoice.com (our "Website") and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

This policy applies to information we collect:

  • On this Website;
  • In email, text, and other electronic messages between you and this Website;
  • Through mobile and desktop applications you download from this Website, if any, which provide dedicated non-browser-based interaction between you and this Website;
  • When you interact with our advertising and applications on third-party websites and services, if those applications or advertising include links to this policy;
  • Through your interactions with our AI-powered voice and chat products, whether embedded on our Website, accessed via phone, or integrated into third-party platforms and communication tools.

This policy does not apply to information collected by:

  • Us offline or through any other means, including on any other website operated by Company or any third party (including our affiliates and subsidiaries); or
  • Any third party (including our affiliates and subsidiaries), including through any application or content (including advertising) that may link to or be accessible from or on the Website.

Please read this policy carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we will treat it. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, your choice is not to use our Website. By accessing or using this Website, you agree to this privacy policy. This policy may change from time to time (see Changes to Our Privacy Policy). Your continued use of this Website after we make changes is deemed to be acceptance of those changes, so please check the policy periodically for updates.

AI Voice and Chat Interactions

When you interact with our AI voice and chat products, we may collect and analyze the following:

  • Transcribed audio and voice recordings;
  • Chat content (user inputs and system responses);
  • Device and session metadata, including timestamps, IP address, browser, session ID;
  • Inferred conversational context including sentiment, urgency, intent classification.

We collect and process this information to:

  • Provide real-time AI-generated responses;
  • Improve and debug system performance;
  • Optimize routing, personalization, and user experience;
  • Support development of services and internal research.

We do not use AI interaction data to train general-purpose AI models without explicit consent. Users will be clearly informed when they are communicating with an AI system.

Fine-Tuning and Model Optimization

We may use de-identified and anonymized transcripts and metadata from your AI interactions to fine-tune and improve JurisVoice’s proprietary AI models, provided such use complies with applicable privacy regulations and does not involve the use of personally identifiable information. This fine-tuning process allows our systems to more accurately understand and respond to similar inquiries in the future, enhancing overall service quality and responsiveness.

Users who wish to opt out of having their anonymized data used for fine-tuning purposes may do so by contacting us at hello@zyrachat.com.

Third-Party AI Service Providers

To operate our AI services, we utilize several third-party providers who process specific types of data strictly on our behalf:

  • Vapi AI and Retell AI (voice call orchestration);
  • Deepgram (speech-to-text processing);
  • OpenAI (language processing);
  • Eleven Labs (text-to-speech synthesis);
  • Twilio (telephony and messaging);
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) (secure infrastructure and hosting).

These providers are contractually bound to process data under confidentiality and data protection requirements. They do not use your personal information for independent purposes.

Data Retention and User Control

We retain personal information, including AI chat and voice logs, as long as necessary for operational, analytical, or legal purposes. Archived data is periodically reviewed and securely stored.

Users may request deletion of their personal information, including AI interaction logs, by contacting hello@zyrachat.com. We may retain anonymized or aggregated data for analytical use.

Our Website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. No one under age 13 may provide any information to or on the Website. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, do not use or otherwise provide any information on this Website. If you believe we might have any information from or about a child under 13, please contact us at hello@zyrachat.com.
