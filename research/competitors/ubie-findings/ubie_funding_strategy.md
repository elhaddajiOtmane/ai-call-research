# How Ubie Health Raised $125M+ — and How to Apply the Playbook

Raising funding beyond $125M like Ubie Health did is not just about a smart idea — it's about building a system and credibility that convinces players like Google and global VC funds to invest. Analyzing Ubie's journey from its founding in 2017 to now reveals the formula they relied on, and how to apply it step by step if you're aiming to build a "fundable" AI project.

## Ubie's Journey: How They Convinced Investors

Ubie didn't raise $125M overnight — they built their journey through 10 gradual funding rounds, starting with Seed and ending at Series C. The core factors that made them a magnet for investors:

**Strict medical credibility (Medical Validation):** The project wasn't founded by engineers alone — physicians (such as Dr. Yoshinori Abe) co-founded it. The system was trained on real, documented medical data, which removed investors' medical and legal concerns.

**Major strategic partnerships:** They partnered with the Mayo Clinic (one of the world's largest medical institutions) to test and train their AI on data from 3 million patients. This partnership alone immediately raises the company's value in any investor's eyes.

**Real traction and numbers:** Their Symptom Checker is used by over 13 million monthly users, deployed across more than 1,800 medical institutions, and generates Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) exceeding $16 million. Investors put money in to accelerate the growth of an already-successful system — not to test an idea that might fail.

## How to Build a Project and Attract Major Funding (Roadmap)

If you want to follow this approach (whether in healthcare or another complex sector), here are the practical stages and steps:

### Stage 1: Build the MVP and Traction (self-funded or pre-seed)

Today's investors (especially in 2026) no longer fund pitch decks alone — they need to see a working initial product with real users.

- **Start with a micro-niche:** Rather than building a massive general platform, focus on solving one real problem (e.g., an AI agent for law firm phone lines to prevent missed calls).
- **Validate the market:** Get your first 10–20 paying customers on a monthly subscription (even if it only covers costs). This proves to investors there's real demand (Product-Market Fit).

### Stage 2: Apply to Incubators and Accelerators

This is the golden gateway to reaching major investors. Joining an accelerator gives you initial seed funding (typically $100K–$500K) in exchange for a small equity stake — but more importantly, it gives you relationships.

- **Top global accelerators:** Y Combinator (YC), Techstars, or specialized programs (such as the Mayo Clinic Platform Accelerate program that Ubie joined).
- **The benefit:** They prepare you for Demo Day, where you pitch your project to hundreds of venture capitalists.

### Stage 3: Target Venture Capital Funds

Once you have a stable product and growing monthly recurring revenue (MoM growth), formal funding rounds begin:

- **Seed round:** To expand a small team and improve the product (typically $1M–$3M). Target early-stage funds such as Hustle Fund or Silicon Valley VCs.
- **Series A and beyond:** This is where major funds and tech companies step in (like the investment Ubie received from Google and Rakuten). At this stage, the funding goal is rapid global expansion and market dominance.

## Strategic Advice for the Current Stage

The difference between you and Ubie right now is resources and time (they started 9 years ago and have a large team). As a solo founder with a limited budget, the best strategy to attract investors later is the "Bootstrap to Venture" approach:

1. Build a Done-For-You AI Agent for a legally uncomplicated sector (such as law firms, veterinary clinics, or real estate) to avoid difficult compliance costs like HIPAA early on.
2. Focus on cash and rapid growth: once you reach recurring revenue (e.g., $10,000–$20,000/month) on your own, your startup becomes very attractive to investors — you'll have proven high efficiency and very low operating costs using automation and AI tools.
