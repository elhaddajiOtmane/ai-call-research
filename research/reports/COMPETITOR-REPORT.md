## Executive Summary

JurisVoice operates in a market where the dominant players (Smith.ai, Ruby.com) charge $300–$2,100/month for generic, self-serve platforms with no local focus. The Queens/Brooklyn immigration law niche is hyper-local, multilingual, and structurally underserved — 42% of law firm calls go unanswered (Clio 2024), and NYC's immigration enforcement surge is driving urgent call volume that solo attorneys cannot staff.

**HVAC is the wrong niche.** ZyraTalk and Signpost are purpose-built for it. Rosie, Allo, Goodcall, and RingCentral AIR compete at $49–$149/month. HVAC owners won't pay $250–$500/month. The gap is already closed.

**Law Firms is the right niche.** The gap is real, widening, and exploitable with a sub-$500 budget through cold email, LinkedIn, bar association outreach, and one free pilot case study.

## Competitor Profiles

### Smith.ai — The Dominant Incumbent
- **Headline:** "Your AI workforce for the front office. Fully staffed, 24/7."
- **Target:** Law firms (~80% of client base), HVAC, healthcare, IT
- **Pricing:** AI-only $95/month; Live hybrid $300–$2,100/month; $10.50–$11.50/overage call
- **Key features:** Hybrid AI+human, 7,000+ integrations (Clio, MyCase, Filevine, ServiceTitan), 400K+ calls/month, 5,000+ clients
- **Gap:** Self-serve only. No white-glove service. No local Queens/Brooklyn focus. No multilingual specialization. Minimum entry $300/month with no setup support.

### Ruby.com — Premium Human Receptionist
- **Headline:** "People-powered, AI-enhanced business call answering."
- **Target:** Attorneys and small law firms explicitly named
- **Pricing:** $250/month (50 min) → $1,725/month (500 min). $3.45–$5/effective minute.
- **Key features:** 24/7 live humans, bilingual, HIPAA-compliant, <10 second answer time
- **Gap:** Human labor at $3.45–$5/effective minute. Immigration attorneys with heavy Spanish volume burn through minute plans fast. No AI autonomy — just humans with AI transcription overlay.

### ZyraTalk — AI Call Center for Home Services
- **Headline:** "Turn Missed Calls Into Revenue Opportunities"
- **Target:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning (core); legal/healthcare claimed but secondary
- **Pricing:** Not disclosed — demo required
- **Key features:** 24/7 AI answering, dispatch board integrations, call transcripts, AI webchat
- **Case studies:** $23,720 plumbing job, $17,464 HVAC call recovered from missed calls
- **Gap:** ZyraTalk owns HVAC narrative. No Queens/Brooklyn positioning. No legal niche depth.

### Signpost — AI + Live for Home Trades
- **Headline:** "Signpost AI + Live Telephone Answering. Customer Communication. Handled."
- **Target:** HVAC contractors (#1 named industry), electricians, plumbers, roofers
- **Pricing:** Three tiers — amounts not disclosed
- **Key features:** Answers in 2 rings, AI + live backup, instant Angi/Thumbtack/Facebook lead response, <7 days implementation

## NICHE DECISION: Law Firms ✅ | HVAC ❌

### Why HVAC is wrong
- 6+ funded competitors at $49–$149/month (Rosie, Allo, Goodcall, RingCentral AIR, ZyraTalk, Signpost)
- HVAC owners anchored to $49–$149/month — JurisVoice $250–$500/month is a 3–5x shock
- ZyraTalk owns the HVAC case study narrative
- Setup fees create friction — trades owners expect SaaS billing
- No ServiceTitan/dispatch integration = no HVAC-native value

### Why Law Firms is right
- 42% of law firm calls go unanswered (Clio 2024 Legal Trends Report)
- NYC immigration enforcement surge (6x ICE detentions vs. 2024) driving urgent call volume
- Smith.ai charges $300–$2,100/month for a platform you configure yourself — JurisVoice is DFY at $250–$500/month
- 79% of legal professionals now use AI daily (up from 19% in 2023)
- Solo Queens/Brooklyn attorneys answer email, attend bar events, trust peer referrals — free acquisition
- Clio/MyCase integration creates switching costs HVAC will never have
