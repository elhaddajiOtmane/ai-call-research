## Chosen sector
**Immigration & Personal Injury law firms — Queens/Brooklyn, NY**

## Why this sector
1. **Existing budget line to displace.** These firms already pay $150–600/mo for human answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai). Replacing a bill is the easiest sale — no "adopt new technology" conversation.
2. **High cost per missed call.** A missed PI call = potential $5,000+ case. NYC personal injury Google Ads CPC is $100–300, so every inbound call is expensive.
3. **Urgent, emotional callers.** If unanswered in ~60 seconds, they call the next firm.
4. **Research already done.** Prospect list of 7 Queens firms exists (see ai_receptionist_strategy_summary.md §5). Switching niches now restarts the clock.

## Positioning rule (kills the main objection)
The agent is **INTAKE ONLY** — never answers legal questions:
- Collects: name, phone, case type, brief description, preferred callback time
- Books consultations into the firm's calendar
- Transfers/escalates urgent matters
- Explicitly refuses legal advice ("The attorney will answer that in your consultation")

This addresses lawyer objections: confidentiality, bar ethics, unauthorized practice of law.

## Backup niche
**HVAC / plumbing / restoration.** Faster decision-makers (owner answers the phone), zero ethics concerns, obvious $500–5,000 loss per missed emergency call.

## Tripwire — when to switch
If after **20 real conversations** with law firm owners there is no paying client and no strong interest, switch to HVAC. No sentimentality, no third option.
